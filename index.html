1

Benchmark Assessment

When you have a new BP to register — this is where everything starts. One form launches the whole process.

📋
Fill in the Benchmark Assessment form
Takes about 5 minutes. You will need the BP name, sport, type of deal, your Sport Owner, and your approval deadline.

You do this

The form asks for: the Business Case name (exactly as it appears in Salesforce — no special characters like /), your Sport Owner, the sport, New Right or Prolongation, scope of rights, approval deadline, and any additional comments.

If someone else is co-owning the deal, select them as Additional Account Owner — they will receive all the same notifications as you.

⚠️ Use your own name as Account Owner. This ensures all notifications come to you and not to someone else.
⚠️ Avoid special characters in the BP name (e.g. use 2026-27, not 2026/27). Slashes and similar characters can cause errors in the file creation.
Open Benchmark Assessment Form →

You receive a confirmation email
Within seconds, you receive an email with your BP file link and Pipeline ID. Save the Pipeline ID — you will need it later.

Your BP file is created automatically in SharePoint
The system creates the file in your personal SharePoint folder using the latest template. No need to create it yourself.

Automatic

The file is already formatted and waiting for you to fill it in. Use the link in your confirmation email to open it directly — or find it in your personal folder in SharePoint. Do not create a separate copy.

Open BP per Account Owner folder →
⚠️ Always use the file created automatically. Never create a separate copy or use an old template — the system tracks the file you were assigned.
▶
⏱️
What if my deadline is under 30 days?
The process pauses automatically and the SRE Team contacts you to align before proceeding.

Automatic

If your approval deadline is less than 30 days away, you receive an alert email and the SRE Team is notified. They will reach out to decide whether to proceed with an exception.

Do not submit the Commercial Review until the SRE Team has confirmed the exception.

Fill Your BP File

Open the file from your confirmation email and complete your section. Then signal to Operations and your Sport Owner that they can start theirs.

✍️
Fill in the Account Owner tab
Open the BP file using the link in your confirmation email. Complete the Account Owner tab — deal terms, match numbers, partnership details, and deal years.

You do this

Fill in everything in the Account Owner tab. Do not modify the other tabs — Operations and your Sport Owner will complete theirs separately.

💡 If the deal includes additional services (e.g. Integrity or other internal services), coordinate internally and fill in the Upstream Services tab with the relevant costs and value.
▶
✅
Mark AO Ready = Yes in the BP Planner
Once your section is complete and context is added, set AO Ready = Yes on your BP row. This triggers the automatic notifications to Operations and your Sport Owner.

You do this Triggers automation

Only set this when your Account Owner tab is fully complete. Setting AO Ready = Yes tells the system you are done and it is time for others to start their sections.

🔔 What happens automatically: Operations (LD, LO, PMOS) and your Sport Owner each receive an email with the BP file link and instructions on what to fill in. You do not need to contact them manually.
Open BP Planner →
▶
⚙️
Operations and Sport Owner complete their sections
Both receive automatic emails with instructions and fill in their respective sections directly in the BP file.

Automatic

Operations (LD, LO, PMOS) fill in the coverage data (match coverage for deal and no-deal scenarios) and downstream costs (venue scouting, remote scouting, technical costs).

Your Sport Owner fills in the Benchmark section with sport-specific data and assessments.

Both receive the file link and instructions automatically. Once they have completed their sections, they will reply to the email they received to let you know they are done — no separate notification from the system.

Commercial Review

Once the BP file is fully complete — all sections filled by AO, Operations, and Sport Owner — submit the Commercial Review form.

📝
Submit the Commercial Review form
Use your Pipeline ID to submit. The form covers Salesforce data, deal context, and commercial and operational insights.

You do this

You will need your Pipeline ID (from the confirmation email after the Benchmark Assessment). The form includes: SF Partnership Name, SF Account, Term, Planned Start, Scope of Rights, Budget Status, 3YP Status, and qualitative context about the deal.

⚠️ Enter your Pipeline ID exactly — no spaces or modifications. A typo will cause the form to fail.
Open Commercial Review Form →

Deal Approval Request

The final step. Once submitted and approved by the Sport Owner, the full deal rationale goes automatically to all relevant stakeholders.

🤝
Align with your Sport Owner before submitting
Before filling in the form, align with your Sport Owner on the deal rationale. They will need to review and approve it before it goes to stakeholders.

You do this

Aligning with the SO before submitting avoids back-and-forth and ensures the submission is solid. The SO will receive the full form content via Teams Approvals once you submit.

▶
📝
Submit the Deal Approval Request form
The form covers the full deal rationale — same content as the DAR email you used to write manually, but now structured and standardized.

You do this

You will need your Pipeline ID. The form has 6 sections:

① Executive Summary — why we recommend this deal
② Sports Portfolio Plan Impact — key risks, opportunities, competitive implications
③ Commercial View — sales categorization, forecasted impact, revisions vs Benchmark
④ AV/DATA Impact — venue coverage, stream speed, operational implications
⑤ Partnership Overview — current rights holder, competitors, viable alternatives
⑥ Embeddedness & Bundled Services — standalone or bundled, additional products

💡 The link to this form arrives automatically in your approval notification email. You can also access it here:
Open Deal Approval Request Form →

▶
🔄
What if the deal scope changes mid-process?
If the scope or financials change at any point, you can create a new version of the BP file without starting from scratch using the BP Amendment form.

You do this Triggers automation

Submit the BP Amendment form with your Pipeline ID, the new BP name, and a brief description of what changed. The system copies your existing file automatically and creates a new version — up to 3 versions (v1, v2, v3) can coexist in SharePoint.

The SRE Team is notified automatically and decides if the evaluation stage needs to be adjusted. All subsequent emails will use the new version link.

⚠️ Never modify the original BP file directly. Always use the BP Amendment form to create a new version — this keeps the history clean and the process tracking intact.
Open BP Amendment Form →
